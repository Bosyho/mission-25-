MISSION 25 — birthday surprise website

HOW TO PREVIEW:
Open index.html in a browser. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

HOW TO PUBLISH:
Upload the complete folder (not just index.html) to a static hosting service such as Netlify.
The assets folder must remain next to index.html.

NOTES:
- This is a static site; no coding is needed to use it.
- The date form opens a WhatsApp share message so the birthday boy can choose your chat.
- The Spotify embed needs an internet connection and may require the Spotify app/account.
- The top SONG button is a reminder; the actual player is on the last page.
- Photos supplied in this conversation are included.
